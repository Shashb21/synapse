# 02: Show design fit, coverage and unanswered requirements for each tactic

## Parent

https://synapse21.atlassian.net/browse/KAN-121

Specification: https://synapse21.atlassian.net/browse/KAN-140

## What to build

From a gap, inspect what each linked tactic contributes and leaves unanswered, with a clear distinction between documented fit, recorded coverage and human review.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Show Fit to this gap with documented matches, known mismatches, unknown information and expected contribution without implying delivered evidence.
- [ ] Present origin, saved judgement, human confirmation and freshness as distinct facts; show unassessed relationships explicitly.
- [ ] Explain unanswered requirements from recorded dimension verdicts and rationales. Missing information remains unknown rather than an asserted mismatch.
- [ ] Adding another tactic does not remove the first tactic’s unanswered requirements. Combined remaining need remains owned by the existing gap coverage engine.
- [ ] Use the same tactic across gaps without copying records or assessments. No residual gaps are created by explanation or display.
- [ ] Keep explanations usable with AI disabled, and respect read-only permissions. Use accessible names and text for meaningful status distinctions.
- [ ] Verify the public read model for independent pair results, unknown versus mismatch, provisional coverage and AI-disabled behavior; verify the visible distinctions in a focused browser journey.
