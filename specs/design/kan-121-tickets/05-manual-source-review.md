# 05: Link sources and review cited discrepancies without AI

## Parent

https://synapse21.atlassian.net/browse/KAN-121

Specification: https://synapse21.atlassian.net/browse/KAN-140

## What to build

Let a reviewer manually attach supporting source information or raise and resolve a cited possible discrepancy against affected records and relationships.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Allow manual supporting links and possible discrepancies for retained documents, including when automatic comparison fails or AI is disabled.
- [ ] Capture a supporting passage/source reference and the affected gap, tactic and relationship assessments; validate that references belong to the same plan.
- [ ] Supporting linkage preserves provenance without confirming or upgrading coverage. Avoid duplicate source links and findings on repeated submission.
- [ ] A pending discrepancy prominently challenges current confirmation while preserving the recorded verdict and current gap status.
- [ ] An authorised human reviewer can dismiss a discrepancy with rationale, or accept a source-backed correction with rationale and explicit review of the underlying change; an incoming claim never replaces confirmed knowledge automatically.
- [ ] Acceptance of a substantive underlying change uses the existing freshness and coverage/revision behavior. Dismissal preserves coverage and records the decision. Neither resolution clears unrelated findings or review reasons.
- [ ] Preserve finding history and human decisions with audit and version checks. Conflicting or stale review submissions fail safely without overwriting a newer decision.
- [ ] Provide keyboard-accessible review and clear errors. Verify pending/dismissed/accepted outcomes, cross-plan rejection, permission denial, AI-disabled operation and stale submissions via public workflows and a focused browser review journey.
