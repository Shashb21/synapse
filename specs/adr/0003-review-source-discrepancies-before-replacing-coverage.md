# Review source discrepancies before replacing confirmed coverage

KAN-121 checks newly parsed claims against relevant gap and tactic records, linking supporting information or raising cited possible discrepancies for human review. A pending discrepancy preserves the recorded coverage verdict and gap status while visibly challenging the assessment; only a human-accepted substantive change makes affected assessments outdated, after which they cannot supply a current Full contribution to Addressed status. This deliberately accepts temporary uncertainty instead of letting incoming documents automatically overwrite confirmed judgements or reopen gaps, while preserving requirement provenance, prior confirmations and approved inventory history through the existing coverage and revision mechanisms.
