# Synapse evidence planning

Language for reviewing evidence gaps and planning how to address them.

## Language

**Evidence status**:
The assessment of how fully existing evidence and activities address an evidence gap: Open, Partially Addressed, or Addressed. Evidence status is distinct from whether a person has reviewed that assessment.
_Avoid_: Review status when referring to evidence coverage

**Review state**:
The state of human review of a gap's evidence assessment. Confirmation means a person has reviewed the assessment; it does not mean the gap is Addressed.
_Avoid_: Addressed when referring only to human confirmation

**Inventory readiness**:
Whether every live gap has been confirmed and every Partially Addressed gap resolved by splitting or rewriting. Outstanding inventory work can coexist with continued work on eligible gaps in later planning stages.
_Avoid_: Priority progress when referring to inventory readiness
