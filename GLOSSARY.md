# Synapse evidence planning

Language for reviewing evidence gaps and planning how to address them.

## Language

**Evidence status**:
The assessment of how fully existing evidence and activities address an evidence gap: Open, Partially Addressed, or Addressed. Evidence status is distinct from whether a person has reviewed that assessment.
_Avoid_: Review status when referring to evidence coverage

**Review state**:
The state of human review of a gap's evidence assessment. Confirmation means a person has reviewed the assessment; it does not mean the gap is Addressed.
_Avoid_: Addressed when referring only to human confirmation

**Inventory readiness**:
Whether every live gap has been confirmed and every Partially Addressed gap resolved by splitting or rewriting. Outstanding inventory work can coexist with continued work on eligible gaps in later planning stages.
_Avoid_: Priority progress when referring to inventory readiness

**Priority confirmation**:
A person's acceptance of a gap's priority judgement. It does not mean the gap is Addressed or that its decision context is complete.
_Avoid_: Gap confirmation when referring to acceptance of priority

**Needs priority review**:
A previously confirmed priority whose supporting planning context has changed and needs another human review. The previous judgement remains available, but it is no longer a confirmed priority.
_Avoid_: Evidence status when referring to an outdated priority judgement

**Evidence needed by**:
The latest date usable evidence must be available to inform a decision. It can be earlier than the decision itself, for example when evidence must be included in advance briefing papers.
_Avoid_: Decision date when referring to the evidence deadline

**Evidence needed**:
The gap statement describing the evidence that is missing. It is distinct from a tactic's specific evidence question.
_Avoid_: Evidence question when referring to the gap statement

**Decision date**:
The date on which the affected decision is expected to be made. It is distinct from the earlier date by which usable evidence may be needed.
_Avoid_: Evidence deadline when referring to the decision date

**Priority rationale**:
The explanation for assigning a gap its priority judgement. It is distinct from the reason a person later gives for changing that judgement or its supporting context.
_Avoid_: Reason for change when referring only to why the current priority is appropriate
