## Cause and decision

The old notice accepted both its target provider and its original provider/mixed value indefinitely. It never recorded whether the saved target had been observed, so it could contradict a later mixed, prior-provider, or unavailable routing state and could reappear after being temporarily hidden.

`ProviderPanel` now owns a two-phase notice lifecycle:

1. After a successful save response, accessible `role="status"` feedback appears immediately while the original routing snapshot remains on screen.
2. The first changed, known routing snapshot may confirm the requested target. Once confirmed, any subsequent saved-routing change clears the notice permanently. A contradictory or unavailable changed snapshot clears it immediately, even before confirmation.

`ControlPanelView` supplies the required `routingSnapshot` prop as serialized persisted configs, or null when their read failed. This captures model, parameter, fallback, and saved-revision changes even when the aggregate provider stays the same. `routeConfigs()` constructs its output in `STAGE_IDS` order. Credentials are excluded from this snapshot; a credential-only refresh retains the notice and recomputes its credential consequence from current metadata.

The bounded render-time state adjustment follows the existing persisted-value synchronization pattern in `RoutingPanel`; no effect, general state-machine utility, or extra public helper was introduced. Existing `postJson` failure handling remains unchanged.
